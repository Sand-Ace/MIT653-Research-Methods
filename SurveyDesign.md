Survey Design – AI in Cybersecurity 

1. Objectives of the Survey  
The primary goal of this survey is to explore the awareness and perception of the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in cybersecurity.

The survey is designed to gather data about the participants' knowledge of AI-based cybersecurity technologies, their perception of the efficiency of AI in detecting cyber threats, their fears about the use of AI in cybersecurity, and their thoughts about the role of AI in cybersecurity in the future.

The gathered data will help our group in conducting the research project regarding the application of AI in cybersecurity.

2. How the Survey Will Be Conducted and Distributed 

An online survey will be used in the study, and it will be done via Google Forms. Online surveys are convenient to conduct because they can be easily distributed.

The link for the survey will be sent to the participants in emails or on social media sites, including university student groups. Participants will be made aware of the research purpose before taking part in the survey.

It is stated in the Week 8 lecture that online surveys can be done via various sites, including Google Forms, Survey Monkey, and Qualtrics.


3. Selected Sample for the Survey 

The survey will seek about 30-50 respondents. The respondents would comprise students in universities, as well as people that have an interest or knowledge in information technology, AI, or cyber security.

The use of the convenience sampling method is applicable, as the respondents would be selected based on availability and willingness to respond.

5. Ethical Considerations 

The completion of the survey by participants will be entirely voluntary. The participants will be made aware of the aim of the research and how their answers will be utilized before participating in the survey.

Personal information that is not relevant to the study will not be collected during the survey. Their answers will be kept private and used strictly for research purposes. Participants will also have the option of declining to participate.

Data collected from the surveys will be kept safely, and access will be restricted to authorized individuals only. This will take care of the three key ethical issues discussed in the lecture.

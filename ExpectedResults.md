# Expected Results

## 1. Overview

This research is expected to provide an insight into how Artificial Intelligence (AI) can improve cybersecurity threat detection and identify the main challenges and limitations of its use.

The results are expected to be based on the responses to the online survey that is estimated to gather the responses of approximately 30-50 participants, including university students and individuals with an interest or knowledge in information technology, AI, or cybersecurity.

## 2. Awareness of AI in Cybersecurity

It is expected that the majority of participants would have at least a basic understanding of what Artificial Intelligence is and how it can be used in cybersecurity. However, the level of knowledge may vary across participants as it will likely depend on their experience with AI, information technology, and cybersecurity.

The survey results are also expected to demonstrate that the level of understanding of AI in cybersecurity is likely to be higher among participants with an increased level of technology proficiency.

## 3. AI for Cyber Threat Detection

The research is expected to indicate that the majority of participants believe that AI can improve cybersecurity threat detection as it can support detecting the anomalies in the data and thus identify potential cyber threats. Participants are also expected to report automation of detection process and faster analysis as the key advantages that AI can bring to cybersecurity.

## 4. Challenges and Limitations

Although AI is estimated to provide significant advantages to cybersecurity, the research is also expected to highlight some concerns that participants have with the use of AI in cybersecurity. Those concerns may include privacy issues, false alerts, incorrect predictions, expenses, and reliance on the training data.

In addition, the research is expected to highlight participants’ concerns that cybercriminals may use AI to launch more sophisticated cyberattacks.

## 5. Overall Expected Outcome

Overall, this research is expected to highlight that AI can provide significant advantages to cybersecurity in terms of threat detection. However, it should not be considered as the replacement for human security analysts but rather as a tool that can support them.

The results of the research are expected to provide further insight into how AI can improve cybersecurity threat detection and outline the benefits along with the key challenges and limitations that AI may introduce to cybersecurity.

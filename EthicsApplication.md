2. Project overview

Research objectives: The primary purpose of this research is to determine the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on cybersecurity. Specifically, the study will focus on the role of AI in assisting organizations in detecting, preventing, and responding to cybersecurity threats. Besides that, it will highlight some of the challenges and risks posed by AI cybersecurity. The specific objectives of this study include:

1. Examining how AI is currently used in cybersecurity;

2. Determining the benefits of using AI in detecting and preventing cyber threats;

3. Identifying challenges and risks associated with using AI in cybersecurity;

4. Establishing participants’ views on the future use of AI in cybersecurity.

Research questions: This study will be guided by the following research questions:

1. How is artificial intelligence used to improve cybersecurity?

2. What are the benefits of using artificial intelligence in detecting and preventing cyber threats?

3. What are the challenges and risks associated with the use of artificial intelligence in cybersecurity?

4. How can artificial intelligence impact the future of cybersecurity?


3. Methodology

Research design: This study will adopt a quantitative research design. A survey will be conducted to collect data from participants on their views, perceptions, and experiences with the use of AI in cybersecurity.

Recruitment: The participants will be recruited on a voluntary basis. The study will target university students and professionals who have an interest in information technology, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity. Prior to recruitment, the participants will be informed about the objectives of this study. Those who agree to participate will be asked to provide their email addresses for sending the questionnaire.

Sample size: The study will use a convenience sampling technique to select 30 participants. The sample size is appropriate for this study since it will enable the collection of adequate data for analysis.

Data collection: The data will be collected using an online survey. The survey will consist of multiple-choice and Likert-scale questions with optional short answer sections. Prior to answering the questions, the participants will be informed about the purpose of this study. Those who agree to participate will provide their consent by responding to the survey. The participants will also be reminded that their responses will not be used for any other purpose besides the research.

Data analysis plan: The data collected using the survey will be analyzed descriptively. The responses to multiple-choice and Likert-scale questions will be analyzed and presented using percentages, tables, and graphs. The responses to the optional short answer questions will be analyzed qualitatively to highlight common themes and ideas related to the research questions.


 4. Ethical considerations

4.1 Voluntary and informed participation: 
The participants will give informed consent to participate in this study. They will be informed about the objectives of the study, as well as how the data will be used. Besides that, they will also be reminded of their right to withdraw from the study at any time before submitting the responses. The first section of the survey will contain an informed consent section. The participants will only be allowed to proceed to the next page after giving their consent.

4.2 Minimizing harm and discomfort to participants: 
This study will cause minimal harm to the participants since it will only collect information related to their knowledge and views on AI cybersecurity. However, some participants might feel uncomfortable when providing responses to certain questions. Therefore, the researcher will endeavor to ensure that the questions are not intrusive. Also, the participants will be allowed to skip any questions they find embarrassing or uncomfortable. Besides that, the survey will not ask the participants to provide passwords to any accounts or sensitive information related to their organizations or government.

4.3 Maintaining confidentiality and privacy: 
The study will ensure that participants’ data is kept confidential. It will also endeavor to maintain privacy by ensuring that the respondents are not identifiable from the published results. The researcher will avoid collecting any personal information from the participants. If any personal information is provided, it will be used only for the purposes of the research. The survey will be set to collect anonymous responses. However, if the need arises to collect identifiable information, the data will be stored securely. Also, the results will not mention the names of the participants.

4.4 Ensuring fairness and respect to all: 
The researcher will treat all participants equally and respectfully. The questions will be phrased in a way that avoids discrimination or stereotyping. The language used in the questions will be polite and professional. All participants must meet the same eligibility criteria, and none of them will be unreasonably favored or treated unfairly. Additionally, the researcher will endeavor to avoid bias when developing the questions and analyzing the results.


4.5 Honesty to colleagues and research integrity:
 The researcher will report the research honestly and avoid misrepresentation of the results. The data and analysis will not be fabricated or manipulated. The final report will describe the research’s limitations, as well as the reasons behind the research choices made. Proper credit will be given to the contributions made by colleagues, the project supervisor, and other researchers whose work was used to develop the research. Also, relevant information obtained from other authors and researchers will be correctly cited using the appropriate reference format.

5. Data management

Storage: The data gathered will be stored electronically in secure digital repositories accessible only to the researchers involved in the study.

The data gathered will be stored for as long as necessary to meet institutional requirements, answer research questions, and meet assessment requirements.

Security: Access to the collected data will be restricted to approved researchers and, where applicable, the project supervisor.

Wherever possible, passwords will be used to restrict access to the collected data. Wherever possible, the collection of personally identifiable information will be avoided.

The data collected from the participants will not be uploaded to a publicly accessible GitHub repository.

Disposal: After the data has been deleted from the storage system, any temporary copies or backups of the data that may have been created will also be deleted if practicable. Alternatively, any hard copies of the data that may have been produced will be disposed of securely.
